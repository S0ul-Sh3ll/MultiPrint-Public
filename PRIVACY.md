# MultiPrint — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026
**App:** MultiPrint: PDF & Image Print (Android package `com.s0ulsh3ll.multiprint`)
**Developer:** Rishabh Dahiya (S0ulSh3ll)
**Contact:** rishabhdahiya.work@gmail.com, or [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/S0ul-Sh3ll/MultiPrint-Public/issues)

This policy explains what information MultiPrint ("the app", "we") handles, why, and who it is shared with. MultiPrint lays out photos, images and PDF pages on sheets of paper and prints them, saves them as a PDF or shares them.

## Summary

- You do **not** need an account. We do **not** run any servers, and we never receive your photos, documents or any personal information.
- Your photos and PDFs are read and laid out **on your phone**. The app never uploads them.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads. See [Ads](#ads) below.

## Your photos and documents

- You choose files with Android's photo picker or file picker, or share them to MultiPrint from another app. MultiPrint gets access only to the files you choose.
- The app reads those files to draw the sheets. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
- To pick up where you left off, the app keeps a small list of the chosen files (their links and names), your layout and your settings in its private storage on the phone. It does not make copies of your photos there.
- Photos you take with the camera from inside the app, and PDFs made for sharing, are kept in the app's temporary cache on the phone. Android can clear that cache at any time.

### Printing, saving and sharing

- **Print** hands the finished sheets to Android's print system and the printer service you pick (for example your printer maker's plugin). What that service does with the job is covered by its own privacy policy.
- **Save PDF** writes the PDF to the `Download/MultiPrint` folder on your phone.
- **Share** sends the PDF to the app you choose in Android's share menu.

These happen only when you tap them.

### Android backup

If you turned on Android's backup, Android may include the app's settings and the list of chosen files in your own Google account backup, as it does for other apps. Your photos and documents themselves are not part of it.

## When the app goes online

Laying out, printing to a nearby printer and saving work without internet. The app itself connects to the internet only to load ads.

### Ads

MultiPrint shows:
- a banner ad from Google AdMob at the top of the screen, and
- a full-screen ad, which may be a video, after a print job has been sent to the printer, while the printer works. No full-screen ad is shown if you cancel the print.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:
- device identifiers (such as the advertising ID),
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- information about ad interactions and the app.

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks you first, and **Ad privacy settings** on the home screen lets you change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See [how Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet, network state | To load ads |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID` and Android's ad services) | Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads, as described above |
| Wake lock, foreground service | Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Android's WorkManager for background tasks |
| Storage (Android 9 and older only) | To save PDFs into your Downloads folder |

The app does not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone permission. When you take a photo from inside the app, Android's own camera app takes it and hands that one picture to MultiPrint.

## Keeping and deleting data

The developer stores no data about you, so there is nothing to request or delete on our side. To remove what the app keeps on your phone, use **Remove all** (the sweep button on the items list), clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. Saved PDFs stay in `Download/MultiPrint` until you delete them. For ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

## Children

MultiPrint is a general-audience printing tool. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above.
